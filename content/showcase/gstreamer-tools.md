The framework ships with tools you can use right away. gst-launch-1.0 builds and runs a pipeline from a text description, gst-play-1.0 plays media, gst-inspect-1.0 lists the available elements, gst-discoverer-1.0 analyses files, and ges-launch-1.0 renders editing timelines.
