GStreamer is a framework for building media pipelines: playback, streaming, WebRTC, capture, editing, analytics and much more, from embedded devices to the cloud. You describe a graph of small components, and GStreamer takes care of the rest: data flow, threading, synchronization, format negotiation, buffer management, seeking and more.

{{< pipeline >}}

Applications take advantage of advances in codec and filter technology transparently, and developers add new codecs and filters by writing a plugin with a clean, generic interface. [Read more ...](/features/)

## Why GStreamer

* **Everywhere:** Linux, Android, Windows, macOS, iOS and the BSDs, using each platform's hardware acceleration.
* **Flexible:** hundreds of plugins for containers, codecs, protocols, filters and machine learning, combined as you need.
* **Fast:** lightweight data passing and multi-threaded pipelines for high performance, with latency management for live and real-time use.
* **Stable:** the 1.x API and ABI have been stable since 2012.
* **Your language:** C, Rust, Python, C++, C#, Java, Go and more through [bindings](/bindings/).
* **Open:** released under the LGPL, developed in the open by a large community. See what it is [used for](/showcase/).
