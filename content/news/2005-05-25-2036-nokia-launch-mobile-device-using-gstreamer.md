---
title: "Nokia launch mobile device using GStreamer"
date: 2005-05-25T20:36:00Z
---

Nokia today announced the [Nokia 770](http://www.nokia.com/nokia/0,,75023,00.html) web tablet which runs GNU/Linux and uses GStreamer for its multimedia. Check out their [developer page](http://www.maemo.org/) for details. This is a great validation of the effort the GStreamer team and we are very happy about GStreamer being used by Nokia for their device.
