date: 2004-02-06T13:40:00Z

The GStreamer team is proud to announce another release of the
GStreamer 0.7 development series.
A lot of work has gone into this release to fix up bugs in current plugins
and core.

For GStreamer 0.7.4, check out
[release notes](/releases/gstreamer/0.7.4.html)
or download the
[tar.gz](/src/gstreamer/gstreamer-0.7.4.tar.gz) or
[tar.bz2](/src/gstreamer/gstreamer-0.7.4.tar.bz2) archive.

For GStreamer Plugins 0.7.4, check out
[release notes](/releases/gst-plugins/0.7.4.html)
or download the
[tar.gz](/src/gst-plugins/gst-plugins-0.7.4.tar.gz) or
[tar.bz2](/src/gst-plugins/gst-plugins-0.7.4.tar.bz2) archive.
