---
title: "GStreamer Plugins 0.6.5 \"EOS\" released"
date: 2004-03-05T11:02:00Z
---

The GStreamer team is proud to announce another release of the
GStreamer Plugins 0.6.x stable series.

This release is a service release, fixing some parallel install issues and
compilation against newer ALSA and mpeg2dec libraries.

Check out
[release notes](/releases/gst-plugins/0.6.5.html)
or download the
[tar.gz](/src/gst-plugins/gst-plugins-0.6.5.tar.gz) or
[tar.bz2](/src/gst-plugins/gst-plugins-0.6.5.tar.bz2) archive.
