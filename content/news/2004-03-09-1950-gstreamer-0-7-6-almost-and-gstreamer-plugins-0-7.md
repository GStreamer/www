[release notes](/releases/gstreamer/0.7.6.html)

[release notes](/releases/gst-plugins/0.7.6.html)
or download the
[tar.gz](/src/gst-plugins/gst-plugins-0.7.6.tar.gz) or
[tar.bz2](/src/gst-plugins/gst-plugins-0.7.6.tar.bz2) archive.
