title: "BeNOW.ca rebroadcasting radio with GStreamer"
date: 2001-10-07T00:00:00Z
---

[BeNOW.ca](http://benow.ca/) has started regularly
rebroadcasting [CJSW](http://cjsw.com/), a Calgary radio
station, using GStreamer. He's got a radio receiver attached to an
SBLive!, using gstreamer-launch to encode and send the audio to an
icecast server. **Update** here's an [mp3 clip](/media/small/benow.mp3) (warning: mpg123 has a cow) and
[another](http://gstreamer.net/media/gstreamer-shoutout2.mp3) of the cast, complete with mention of GStreamer &lt;g&gt;
