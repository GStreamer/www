date: 2014-10-20T13:00:00Z

The GStreamer team is pleased to announce the new the stable 1.4
release of gst-editing-services, gst-python, gnonlin, and GstValidate.
The 1.4 release series is adding new features on top of the 1.0 and 1.2
series and is part of the API and ABI-stable 1.x release series of the
GStreamer multimedia framework.

Check out the release notes for
[gst-editing-services](/releases/gst-editing-services/1.4.0.html),
[gnonlin](/releases/gnonlin/1.4.0.html),
[gst-python](/releases/gst-python/1.4.0.html),
[gst-validate](/releases/gst-validate/1.4.0.html),
and download tarballs for
[gst-editing-services](/src/gst-editing-services/gstreamer-editing-services-1.4.0.tar.xz),
[gnonlin](/src/gnonlin/gnonlin-1.4.0.tar.xz),
[gst-python](/src/gst-python/gst-python-1.4.0.tar.xz).
[gst-validate](/src/gst-validate/gst-validate-1.4.0.tar.xz).
