date: 2002-10-29T01:26:00Z

David Schleef has been developing a LGPL Flash rendering libary which in turn the GStreamer Flash plugins are using. This library is independent of GStreamer as such, only non-system dependency being libart. So if Flash rendering is your thing be sure to check out the new [libSwfdec](http://swfdec.sourceforge.net/) homepage.
