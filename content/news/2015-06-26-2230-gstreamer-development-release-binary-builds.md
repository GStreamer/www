---
title: "GStreamer development release binary builds"
date: 2015-06-26T22:30:00Z
---

Pre-built binary images of the 1.5.2 development release of GStreamer are now
available for Windows 32/64-bit, iOS and Mac OS X and Android.

The builds are available for download from:
[Android](/data/pkg/android/1.5.2/),
[iOS](/data/pkg/ios/1.5.2/),
[Mac OS X](/data/pkg/osx/1.5.2/) and
[Windows](/data/pkg/windows/1.5.2/).
