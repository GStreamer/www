The GStreamer Conference team is pleased to announce that the preliminary list
of talks, abstracts, and speaker biographies is now available for
this year's lineup of talks and speakers, covering again an exciting range of
topics!

The **GStreamer Conference 2026** will take place on **10-11 October 2026
in Prague, Czech Republic**, followed by a hackfest.

Details about the conference, hackfest and how to register can be found on the
[conference website](/conference/2026/).
