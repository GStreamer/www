---
title: "GStreamer moves to Freedesktop.org"
date: 2003-12-21T13:49:00Z
---

In order to send out a strong signal that we are serious about our cross-desktop commitment and to get improved CVS services we are moving our CVS repository to freedesktop.org. We hope that this move will be an positive milestone in our continued development. For details for how to access freedesktop CVS check out the information in our [developers](/dev) page.
