---
title: "IRC Channel has moved from Freenode to OFTC"
date: 2021-06-15T08:30:00Z
---

Due to the widely reported issues at the Freenode IRC network, the
official GStreamer discussion IRC channel has moved to #gstreamer on
the OFTC IRC network alongside other Freedesktop projects.

You can connect to it with your existing [IRC client](irc://irc.oftc.net/gstreamer),
or using [Matrix](https://matrix.to/#_oftc_%23gstreamer:matrix.org) which
has a browser client and native apps for all platforms.

For more information, please see
[the mailing list announcement](https://lists.freedesktop.org/archives/gstreamer-devel/2021-June/078449.html).
