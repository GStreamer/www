---
title: "GStreamer 0.6.4"
date: 2003-10-14T06:34:00Z
---

The GStreamer team is very happy to announce the release GStreamer 0.6.4. -the latest update in our ABI stable 0.6.x release series. There are many small and minor fixes in this release to enhance your multimedia pleasure.

In related news, Scott Wheeler has made Suse packages of GStreamer which you can use with his very cool Juk music player and manager for KDE. You'll find Scott's packages also listed in the [download section](http://www.gstreamer.net/download/).
