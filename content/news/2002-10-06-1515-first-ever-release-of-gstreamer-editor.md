---
title: "First-Ever Release of GStreamer Editor"
date: 2002-10-06T15:15:00Z
---

After two years of (sporadic) development, [gst-editor](/modules/gst-editor.html), GStreamer's graphical
pipeline editor, has finally been released
into the wild. The code is very alpha and is prone to eating small mammals,
so don't use it near your cat. Please report any reproduceable bugs to [Gnome
bugzilla](<http://bugzilla.gnome.org/buglist.cgi?product=GStreamer&component=gst-editor>).
