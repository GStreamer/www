---
title: "GStreamer Core and Plugins 1.0.8 bug-fix release"
date: 2013-07-12T23:00:00Z
---

The GStreamer team is pleased to announce another bug-fix release for the
new API and ABI-stable 1.x series of the GStreamer multimedia
framework.

Check out the release notes for
[GStreamer core](/releases/gstreamer/1.0.8.html),
[gst-plugins-base](/releases/gst-plugins-base/1.0.8.html),
[gst-plugins-good](/releases/gst-plugins-good/1.0.8.html),
[gst-plugins-ugly](/releases/gst-plugins-ugly/1.0.8.html),
[gst-plugins-bad](/releases/gst-plugins-bad/1.0.8.html), or
[gst-libav](/releases/gst-libav/1.0.8.html),
or download tarballs for
[gstreamer](/src/gstreamer/gstreamer-1.0.8.tar.xz),
[gst-plugins-base](/src/gst-plugins-base/gst-plugins-base-1.0.8.tar.xz),
[gst-plugins-good](/src/gst-plugins-good/gst-plugins-good-1.0.8.tar.xz),
[gst-plugins-ugly](/src/gst-plugins-ugly/gst-plugins-ugly-1.0.8.tar.xz),
[gst-plugins-bad](/src/gst-plugins-bad/gst-plugins-bad-1.0.8.tar.xz), or
[gst-libav](/src/gst-libav/gst-libav-1.0.8.tar.xz),

Check the [release announcement mail](http://lists.freedesktop.org/archives/gstreamer-devel/2013-July/041975.html) for a
list of changes.

Also available are binaries for [Android](/data/pkg/android/1.0.8/),
[Mac OS X](/data/pkg/osx/1.0.8/) and
[Windows](/data/pkg/windows/1.0.8/). Check the
[release announcement mail](http://lists.freedesktop.org/archives/gstreamer-devel/2013-July/041991.html) of
the binaries for more details.
