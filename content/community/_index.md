**NOTE:** The mailing lists are being phased out and most of the
discussions now happen on the [GStreamer Discourse](https://discourse.gstreamer.org/)
instance. Users who prefer to use e-mail can configure their Discourse
accounts to behave like a mailing-list, more information [here](https://discourse.gstreamer.org/t/using-discourse-via-email/109/2).

Announcements, including new releases, will be posted on the [News and Announcements
Discourse category](https://discourse.gstreamer.org/c/news/5). Users can subscribe to that category.

There are currently eight mailing lists for GStreamer:

* **gstreamer-announce**:
  *[Subscribe](http://lists.freedesktop.org/mailman/listinfo/gstreamer-announce)
  | [Archives](http://lists.freedesktop.org/archives/gstreamer-announce/)*

  Used for announcements of new versions, features, etc.

* **gstreamer-devel**:
  *[Subscribe](http://lists.freedesktop.org/mailman/listinfo/gstreamer-devel)
  | [Archives](http://lists.freedesktop.org/archives/gstreamer-devel/)*

  Any and all discussions about GStreamer, GStreamer integration,
  application development with GStreamer and plugin development with
  GStreamer. Ask here if you have problems with GStreamer. Please submit
  patches and bug reports in [GitLab](https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/gstreamer/gstreamer/)
  and not on the mailing list.

* **gstreamer-commits**:
  *[Subscribe](http://lists.freedesktop.org/mailman/listinfo/gstreamer-commits)*

  git commit notifications

* **gstreamer-openmax**:
  *[Subscribe](http://lists.freedesktop.org/mailman/listinfo/gstreamer-openmax)
  | [Archives](http://lists.freedesktop.org/archives/gstreamer-openmax/)*

  Development of GStreamer wrappers for OpenMax components.

* **gstreamer-android**:
  *[Subscribe](http://lists.freedesktop.org/mailman/listinfo/gstreamer-android)
  | [Archives](http://lists.freedesktop.org/archives/gstreamer-android/)*

  Development of GStreamer for Android integration.

* **gstreamer-embedded**:
  *[Subscribe](http://lists.freedesktop.org/mailman/listinfo/gstreamer-embedded)
  | [Archives](http://lists.freedesktop.org/archives/gstreamer-embedded/)*

  Discussion of building, optimising, developing and using GStreamer on embedded devices.

* **gstreamer-bugs**:
  *Defunct*

  This mailing list is no longer in use. You can subscribe to notification about issues
  and merge requests in [GitLab](https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/gstreamer/gstreamer/)).
