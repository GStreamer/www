## 22-23 October 2013 · Edinburgh International Conference Centre · Edinburgh, Scotland, UK

For the fourth GStreamer Conference we're back in Europe. This year we will be co-hosting with the Embedded Linux Conference Europe, LinuxCon Europe, the Automotive Linux Summit and the Linux Kernel Summit. The event will be held at the Edinburgh International Conference Centre (EICC) in Edinburgh, Scotland.

## Details

The GStreamer Conference is a conference for developers, decision-makers, and anyone else interested in the GStreamer multimedia framework and open source multimedia.

This year we will provide more time and opportunity for networking and socialising.

## Schedule

The [conference schedule](/conference/2013/schedule.html) and is now available.

## Call for Papers

The call for papers is now closed and talk proposals can no longer be submitted.

## Who should join?

Anyone interested in open source multimedia is encouraged to join us at the GStreamer Conference 2013. Whether you are a hobbyist or a student interested in the field of multimedia, or working for a company using or looking to use GStreamer in their products, this is the perfect event for you to network at and to learn more about the latest technological breakthroughs in and around GStreamer. The talks will target both developers and managers alike.

## Registration

Registration is now open: **[sign up for the GStreamer Conference](http://www.regonline.com/gstreamerconference2013)**

There will be separate registration options for both professionals and for students and hobbyists:

|                  |                         |
|------------------|-------------------------|
| Professional     | 250 USD (until 31 July) |
| Professional     | 350 USD (from 1 August) |
| Student/hobbyist | 100 USD                 |

For those uncertain about whether to register as a professional or hobbyist, the general rule is that if your company pays for you or sponsors you, you are a professional, and if you personally pay you are a student or hobbyist.

Speakers will receive a discount code, so if you have submitted a talk or plan to submit a talk you may want to wait with registration until a decision has been made as to whether your proposal has been accepted.

Sponsors will receive a number of free registrations as part of the [sponsorship package](/documents/gstreamer-sponsor-2013.pdf).

## Hotels

### Hilton Edinburgh Grosvenor Hotel

The Linux Foundation has reserved a block of rooms at a discounted rate of £89 (€105/\$135) per night for a single room and £99 (€115/\$150) for a double/twin room (incl. taxes and breakfast) at the [Hilton Edinburgh Grosvenor Hotel](http://www3.hilton.com/en/hotels/united-kingdom/hilton-edinburgh-grosvenor-hotel-EDNGRHN/index.html), which GStreamer Conference attendees can use as well. The hotel is about 5-10 minutes walk from the Edinburgh International Conference Centre where the conference will take place. This rate is available only up to 60 days before arrival, and there are also other conferences such as LinuxCon going on in the same week, so make sure to book as early as possible.

[Reserve your room online by clicking here](http://www.hilton.com/en/hi/groups/personalized/E/EDNGRHN-GLINA-20131019/index.jhtml?WT.mc_id=POG).

### Travelodge Edinburgh Central Rose Street and Travelodge Edinburgh Central Queen Street

There are two [Travelodge](http://www.travelodge.co.uk/) budget hotels about 1 mile or 20 minutes walk away from the conference centre. Advanced booking rates were around £26 per night at the time of writing (early July).

### Ibis Edinburgh Centre

The [Ibis Edinburgh Centre](http://ibishotel.ibis.com/gb/hotel-2039-ibis-edinburgh-centre-royal-mile/index.shtml) is about 1 mile or 20 minutes walk away from the conference centre, and advanced booking rates for a single room were about £65 per night at the time of writing (early July).

### Other hotels

Edinburgh is an international tourist destination and as such has many hotels and hostels in all price categories. The above is merely a small selection, you can find more using the usual booking engines.
