---
title: "GStreamer Conference"
type: "conference"
---

The GStreamer Conference is the yearly meeting of the people who build and use GStreamer. It has talks about the framework, its plugins and the products made with it, and plenty of time to talk with the maintainers. A hackfest usually follows.
