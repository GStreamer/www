## 10-11 October 2026 · Prague, Czech Republic

![Group picture of the GStreamer Conference 2026 attendees](/images/gstconf-2026-group-picture.webp)

![Prague](/images/banner-prague.jpg)

The **14th GStreamer Conference** brings the community together in **Prague, Czech Republic**, on **Saturday and Sunday 10-11 October 2026**: two days of talks, demos and hallway conversations with the people who build GStreamer and ship it in real products. A **GStreamer hackfest** follows on **Monday and Tuesday 12-13 October 2026**.

It runs right after the **[Embedded Linux Conference Europe](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/embedded-linux-conference-europe/)**, and the weekend dates make it easy for ELCE attendees to stay on and join us.

## Details

The GStreamer Conference is where developers, community members, decision-makers, industry partners, researchers and students meet to share what is new in the GStreamer multimedia framework and in open source, cross-platform multimedia.

## Who should attend?

If open source, cross-platform multimedia interests you, the GStreamer Conference 2026 is for you.

Whether you are a hobbyist, app developer, researcher or student, or you work for a company that uses GStreamer or plans to, this is the place to learn about the latest technical developments and project news, to meet the developers behind them, and to connect with the wider community.

## City

This year's conference will take place in **[Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague), Czech Republic**.

*Getting there*

**By train** You can travel directly by train to Prague from many cities, such as Berlin, Budapest, Bratislava, Copenhagen, Dresden, Hamburg, Kraków, Linz, Munich, Vienna, and Warsaw. Many more are just one change away.

**By air** Prague is served by a single international airport: Václav Havel Airport Prague (PRG), with [direct flights](https://www.prg.aero/en/route-map) to more than 160 destinations, and also easily reachable through all major European hubs.

**Entry requirements, Visas** The Czech Republic is part of the European "Schengen area". Many international visitors, including most EU, EEA, Swiss US, Canadian, Australian, Japanese and South Korean citizens, will neither need a Visa nor prior travel authorization to enter, only a valid passport. A European travel authorization scheme ([ETIAS](https://travel-europe.europa.eu/etias)) is planned for late 2026 / early 2027, but this is not in operation yet, and no start date has been announced yet, so we think it is unlikely to come into force before the conference.

See [Visit Czechia - Entry to the Country](https://www.visitczechia.com/en-us/travel-info/entry-to-the-country) for links to the list of countries whose citizens do need to apply for a Visa and which do not.

## Code of Conduct and Anti-Harrassment Policy {#coc}

### Policy

The GStreamer project and the conference organisers are dedicated to providing a harassment-free conference experience for everyone. We do not tolerate harassment of conference participants in any form.

Harassment includes, but is not limited to, offensive verbal comments, sexual images in public spaces, deliberate intimidation, stalking, following, harassing photography or recording, sustained disruption of talks or other events, inappropriate physical contact, and unwelcome sexual attention. Participants asked to stop any harassing behaviour are expected to comply immediately. We expect participants to follow these rules at all conference venues, hackfest venues, and conference-related social events.

If a participant engages in harassing behaviour, the conference organisers and/or their delegates may take any action they deem appropriate, including warning the offender or expulsion from the conference. If you are being harassed, notice that someone else is being harassed, or have any other concerns, please contact a conference organiser or member of the code of conduct committee below immediately.

### Reporting Breaches

Breaches of the code of conduct should be reported either by e-mail via <code-of-conduct@gstreamer.org>.

See [here](https://www.x.org/wiki/XorgFoundation/Policies/HarassmentDetails/) for implementation details and guidelines.

This anti-harassment policy is based on the [XDC 2024 code of conduct](https://indico.freedesktop.org/event/4/page/26-code-of-conduct) which in turn is based on the example policy from the [Geek Feminism wiki](http://geekfeminism.wikia.com/wiki/Conference_anti-harassment/Policy), created by the [Ada Initiative](http://adainitiative.org/) and other volunteers.
